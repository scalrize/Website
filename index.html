Why Scalrize exists

A founder who got tired of the chaos.

Running multiple businesses, I kept hitting the same wall, too many tools, too much manual work, and no single place to see what was actually happening. A CRM here, an invoicing app there, a chat tool for customers, and a spreadsheet holding it all together.

I started deploying and configuring the best available AI tools to solve my own problem, and when it worked, other business owners wanted the same thing.

So we turned it into a service, and now we deploy it for companies that are done duct-taping their operations together.
